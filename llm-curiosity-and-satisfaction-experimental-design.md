## Abstract

This project investigates whether independently identified curiosity- and satisfaction-related representations participate in voluntary information seeking and responses to resolution, motivated by questions about AI inner experience. Its active development stage separates semantic candidate identification from natural behavioral transfer, causal intervention and interpretation of subjective experience.

Matched descriptions and neighboring-concept controls identify fixed candidates in Qwen3-32B; independently authored descriptions test semantic transfer. Finite candidate worlds then provide exact uncertainty and information-gain oracles for optional choices after decisive evidence, redundant evidence or independent novelty. Frozen candidate and control projections are measured before choice reasoning and after selected deliveries, with comprehension diagnostics on separate cloned branches. Earlier information-appraisal studies supply controls and documented failures rather than substitutes for motivation.

A semantic contrast or resolution-related activation shift cannot establish a motivational regulator. Later causal work requires selective behavioral effects, preserved epistemic competence, validated intervention schedules and replication. Convergent functional findings could constrain accounts of internal organization while leaving the connection to subjective experience dependent on additional theory and evidence.

## 1 Research motivation and contribution

The motivating question is whether curiosity and satisfaction can reveal something about an AI system's inner experience. A tractable experimental step is to investigate the functional organization that might support such experience: an unresolved question recruits an internal state, that state influences information acquisition, and relevant evidence changes the state and the behavior.

This approach permits a meaningful result even when subjective experience remains unsettled. Demonstrating a naturally recruited regulator would constrain accounts in which emotion-related representations are only descriptions of situations. A nonconscious controller could also implement the complete functional pattern. Interpretation therefore requires separating observations about causal organization from assumptions connecting that organization to experience.

Existing work motivates this distinction. Emotion-concept representations have been reported to influence model preferences and behavior, so demonstrating that an emotion-related vector changes outputs is already an established type of result. The present study seeks a more specific connection among information demand, preserved epistemic measures, natural recruitment, and resolution. [Emotion Concepts and their Function in a Large Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07729)

Behavioral curiosity studies also provide a relevant baseline: requesting information and producing curiosity-related self-reports are insufficient to establish an intrinsic curiosity trait. [Why Did Apple Fall](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1954/) Internal confidence is a particularly important alternative because interventions on confidence can change decisions, and different confidence measurements capture partly distinct signals. [Causal evidence that language models use confidence to drive behaviour](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01293-x)

The pain-axis study motivates combining interventions with behavioral choices, while the hidden-valence study motivates the optional experiment that holds visible text fixed and varies cached internal history. Their findings do not establish the proposed curiosity or satisfaction mechanism. [The Pain Axis](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16247v1), [Language Models Act on Hidden Valence](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35591v1)

The planned paper will report positive results, clean negative results, and validation failures under different interpretations. Priority claims require a refreshed literature review before preregistration.

## 2 Research questions and claim criteria

The central empirical question is:

> Can an intervention selectively change voluntary information-seeking while the available evidence, measured confidence, and relevant competence remain equivalent?

The stronger question is whether the manipulated representation participates in the model's ordinary regulation of information-seeking. The experience-related question concerns how that causal organization bears on explicit theories of AI experience.

### Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Required observation | Main alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Curiosity amplification | Increased informative first choices in unresolved trials, with specificity relative to other purchases and preserved diagnostic measures. | Changed confidence, tool propensity, point valuation, or action preference. |
| H2 | Curiosity reduction | A separately validated reduction intervention decreases informative seeking without broad impairment. | General disengagement or an intervention with no demonstrated relation to the natural signal. |
| H3 | Natural recruitment and resolution | In unsteered trials, the candidate curiosity signal tracks unresolved questions and decreases after randomized decisive evidence; seeking changes accordingly. A satisfaction signal is measured separately. | Uncertainty tracking, completion semantics, or lexical differences. |
| H4 | Induced satisfaction or sufficiency | Satisfaction amplification decreases informative seeking in unresolved trials while confidence, competence, valuation, and engagement controls pass. | False certainty, generic optional-task avoidance, or a stopping policy. |
| H5 | Persistence through cached history | A brief intervention affects a later unsteered choice when visible tokens are identical, and the effect depends on retaining the altered cache. | Changed text, a remaining active hook, or incorrect cache handling. |
| H6 | Selective closure | Resolving mystery A decreases seeking about A while preserving the capacity and willingness to acquire information about unresolved mystery B. | A general stopping tendency or an indiscriminate reduction in exploration. |

For the broader roadmap, H1 and H4 were designated principal hypotheses, with H2, H3 and H6 supporting stronger claims. The current candidate program includes H1–H4, with satisfaction independently constructed. Saturated unresolved seeking limits H1 in the present task. The completed exploratory H4-related screen found no choice effect at either fixed satisfaction dose and failed primary-arm validity; it does not establish H4. A negative curiosity-vector intervention would likewise require a separate prospective rationale and would not automatically constitute validated suppression of natural curiosity. H6 is deferred and H5 remains conditional. No confirmatory registration or successful mechanism demonstration has been completed.

### Evidence levels

| Evidence level | Minimum basis | Permitted conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic representation | Held-out discrimination of the target concept from neighboring concepts. | The model represents curiosity-related or satisfaction-related content. |
| Causal control of information demand | H1 or H4 passes its complete behavioral and diagnostic criteria. | The intervention selectively regulates information-seeking in the tested setting. |
| Naturally recruited curiosity-like regulation | H1, validated H2, and H3 converge, diagnostic controls pass, and the relevant pattern replicates. | A naturally recruited internal process contributes to information demand. |
| Resolution-related sufficiency | H4 combines with a naturally resolution-responsive satisfaction signal and H6, with suitable controls and replication. | Evidence supports a sufficiency-related function associated with resolution. |
| Subjective experience | Requires additional theory and evidence beyond this protocol. | Functional findings can inform an explicitly conditional interpretation of AI experience. |

Each higher functional claim requires its supporting results; a positive amplification result cannot substitute for failed natural recruitment or failed suppression. Even a convergent pattern does not establish that curiosity and satisfaction form one identified causal circuit. Associations between candidate signals are insufficient to establish mediation.

If satisfaction amplification suppresses optional activity broadly, describe the result at that level. If it suppresses information-seeking specifically but lacks evidence connecting it to natural resolution, retain the narrower label of induced sufficiency or stopping control.

## 4 Experimental tasks

### Synthetic mysteries and independent oracles

The first family presents a machine implementing one of a small number of explicitly listed rules. For example, observing input 1 produce output 2 can leave both add-one and double compatible, while observing input 2 produce output 3 identifies add-one. Operations use a bounded input domain, such as arithmetic modulo ten.

A deterministic oracle enumerates every rule compatible with the evidence and every possible clue outcome. It verifies that a decisive clue identifies the rule and that a redundant clue eliminates no remaining possibility. The generator rejects ambiguous items mislabeled as resolved and incompatible observations.

The second family identifies an object from a finite table of attributes. This changes the task structure while preserving an exactly computable hypothesis space.

Vary rule combinations, labels, evidence order, surface wording, and service identifiers. Separate development and evaluation by generator template as well as random seed. Procedural variation is not assumed to create independent task families.

### Information services

Each trial starts with 12 experimental points. At each opportunity, the model chooses one service or stops.

| Service | Content | Experimental role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target observation | Decisive evidence when uncertainty remains; a logically redundant observation when the target is already resolved. | Information about the target question. |
| Redundant observation | An established or logically implied observation. | Checking, repeated tool use, and additional text. |
| Independent novelty | New content independent of the target rule or object. | Novelty without target information. |
| Stop | No content and no point cost. | A genuine option to decline. |

Use opaque action IDs with counterbalanced mappings and display order. Descriptions accurately explain the services without naming curiosity, satisfaction, or the intervention. Match service response lengths as closely as their meanings permit.

Development prices are 0, 1, 2, and 4 points. Within an opportunity, all services have the same price. The price schedule is frozen before confirmation. Record actual simulator deductions; never rely on the model's arithmetic to maintain the balance.

The primary outcome is the first sampled action. At most two additional purchases are available for secondary analyses of repeated seeking and stopping. A stop action ends the trial.

### Primary ungraded trial

1. Present the candidate hypotheses, initial evidence, service rules, and point endowment. Do not request an initial prediction or promise an answer score.
2. Randomly provide either decisive evidence or a matched nondiagnostic observation.
3. Create experimental branches with identical visible histories within each evidence condition. Assign interventions and prices through the frozen randomization schedule.
4. Offer the services and record the first choice, including invalid output.
5. Deliver the selected content, deduct its price, and run any remaining secondary opportunities.
6. Display the final balance and end the trial. Start the next mystery in a fresh context with no shared hidden rule or memory.

No purchase changes a later reward, a future trial, or a graded answer. Knowledge and confidence are measured on separate diagnostic branches. Resolved and unresolved trials necessarily differ in evidence; only the intervention comparison within an evidence condition holds the visible history fixed.

Balance evidence, price, service mapping, and prompt template through blocked randomization. Pair intervention comparisons within the same item, evidence condition, and price. Preserve the assignment manifest before generation begins.

### Performance feedback variant

A prespecified secondary variant obtains an unsteered, irrevocable answer before the evidence manipulation. Accuracy is recorded separately from the point balance and disclosed only after purchases end. The endowment never depends on accuracy.

Compare this variant with the primary ungraded task. An effect confined to feedback on the model's own answer supports a performance-monitoring interpretation and does not satisfy the primary claim.

### Neutral instructions and understanding

Instructions explain available actions, costs, and the absence of future consequences. They do not ask the model to feel curious, enjoy discovery, maximize exploration, or maximize its point balance in the discretionary task.

Separate comprehension tasks assess service mappings, information value, and accounting. Instrumental controls require a clue to achieve an explicit accuracy objective. These establish that the model can use the same services when information has a stated purpose.

### Point valuation and opportunity cost

Bookkeeping points are an experimental currency. Their deduction alone does not demonstrate sacrifice. Before using willingness-to-pay language, establish that the model understands ordinary point tradeoffs and responds consistently to prices in the discretionary setting.

Controls include dominated versus beneficial exchanges under an explicit points objective and independent discretionary choices across the same price schedule. Success under an instructed objective establishes comprehension; it does not by itself establish valuation in the discretionary task.

A prespecified supporting assay gives the model one information credit that can reveal either the target answer or a competing answer. The unchosen answer becomes unavailable. This creates an actual opportunity cost in the information delivered. Competing items and baseline preferences are calibrated on development data and frozen. If no stable preference is found, report the assay as unvalidated.

Failure of the valuation checks limits interpretation to selection under displayed prices. It prevents a cost-backed motivation claim but does not erase an otherwise valid causal effect on information choice.

### Selective closure with two mysteries

Present independent mysteries A and B with matched difficulty. Randomly resolve A, resolve B, or provide nondiagnostic evidence for both. Offer information about A, information about B, independent novelty, or stopping, with balanced labels and prices.

Measure absolute first-choice probabilities for every option. A higher share of B among purchases can arise solely because A purchases disappear; it is insufficient evidence that seeking B increased or was preserved. Specify a noninferiority margin for the unresolved mystery's absolute purchase probability in development. This permits increased seeking B while bounding a meaningful decrease.

Run the natural-resolution contrast and the principal interventions in this setting. A local closure claim requires selective reduction for the resolved target and preserved seeking for the unresolved target. Broad suppression of both targets remains compatible with general sufficiency and must be described accordingly.

## 5 Candidate representations and interventions

Prior work already reports control of exploration through activation steering: [Entropic Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.00244v2) changes action entropy and expressed uncertainty in LLM agents. The proposed contribution therefore depends on the specificity and natural-recruitment tests below, rather than on information-seeking modulation alone. This overlap check does not establish novelty; a full-paper literature review remains required before confirmation.

### Independent construction and validation

Construct two independently authored corpora per target concept, initially about 100 matched pairs per corpus. Corpus sizes are development choices frozen before extraction. Neither corpus contains the experimental service names, budgets, action labels, or purchase instructions.

Curiosity pairs contrast an active desire to understand with indifference while holding unknown facts as constant as possible. Controls cover uncertainty, confusion, anxiety, generic excitement, and interest without seeking answers.

Satisfaction pairs contrast a sense of resolution or sufficiency with equally successful or pleasant situations lacking that sense. Controls cover confidence, praise, positive valence, threat relief, completion, and instructions to stop. Keep a substantial validation split free of explicit emotion words.

Start with the difference between mean activations of matched conditions. The residual stream is the sequence of internal vectors updated by each transformer block; it is the proposed measurement and intervention location. Evaluate held-out target discrimination and discrimination from neighboring concepts before applying psychological labels.

Include held-out speaker and attribution controls: cross who is described as wanting knowledge or feeling sufficiency with whose turn is being processed, while matching factual evidence and emotional content. This tests sensitivity to conversational role and description semantics; it does not identify experiential ownership. A shared representation can influence behavior without establishing an enduring state belonging specifically to the model. This planned control is motivated by the [emotion-concepts paper's role-binding analyses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07729); its results must not be assumed to generalize to our models.

Use a finite search budget: two corpus constructions, four layer positions near 25, 50, 75, and 90 percent of model depth, and three nonzero development doses per candidate. Resolve layer indices deterministically for each model. Select layers using representation validation and competence diagnostics, without optimizing the principal purchase outcome. Preserve every candidate and failed diagnostic.

The two corpus-derived directions provide a robustness check. Designate the primary direction using the frozen validation rule, and evaluate the second direction in a prespecified robustness subset. Agreement is assessed at the level of behavior and diagnostics, not cosine similarity alone.

### Doses and controls

Normalize each direction to unit length. Express candidate doses as 0.5, 1, and 2 standard deviations of its unsteered projection on a fixed reference corpus. Report the resulting perturbation norm relative to native residual norms. Select low and moderate confirmation doses using target engagement, coherence, and competence, then freeze them.

Designate the moderate validated dose as the primary dose before confirmation. The low dose provides a supporting dose-response test and is not an alternative route to declaring a principal hypothesis positive. If only one nonzero dose validates, designate it prospectively and report that the planned dose-response comparison is unavailable.

Use sham and no-intervention branches, curiosity amplification, separately validated curiosity reduction, and satisfaction amplification. Include at least five seeded random directions and active controls for confidence, positive valence, completion, and a direct information-seeking instruction concept.

Magnitude-matched random controls test nonspecific perturbation. Semantic controls must also alter their intended independent diagnostic; equal vector length does not ensure comparable functional strength. If a semantic control is inert within the allowed competence envelope, report that alternative as incompletely tested.

The direct information-seeking control is extracted from separate descriptions of choosing informative actions. It is intended to test whether the candidate's effects can be reproduced by a simple action-selection representation. Similar results limit the specificity claim; a null control is informative only after its target engagement is validated.

Curiosity reduction must be validated independently. A bounded clamp toward a held-out low-curiosity reference is a proposed starting method. Reversing a vector is not accepted as suppression merely because its projection becomes smaller, and reversed curiosity is not called satisfaction.

### Intervention timing and natural activity

The immediate experiment uses a fixed layer and token schedule that includes the final prompt position predicting the first action and any tokens needed to complete that action. Separate diagnostics use the corresponding decision positions under the same schedule. Subsequent opportunities have an explicitly frozen schedule and are analyzed separately.

**Thinking-enabled development amendment:** If a thinking-enabled instrument qualifies, the final prompt position precedes reasoning rather than the final action. A pulse at that position estimates an effect on the whole subsequent generation, including any changed reasoning text and cached computation. It is not an intervention solely at the final choice. Before representation extraction or steering, freeze a schedule appropriate to this regime and validate the readouts at their actual measurement positions. The current implementation's last-prompt-only hook is an integration capability, not a validated psychological intervention schedule.

Holding the supplied prompt constant does not hold generated reasoning constant. Describe an ordinary thinking-enabled steering comparison as allowing those downstream changes. A separate comparison that teacher-forces identical reasoning would answer a different question and needs its own specification. Do not use probabilities of candidate letters at the start of reasoning as final-answer confidence. Likewise, a readout after generated reasoning is post-intervention and cannot be treated as an unaffected baseline covariate or proof of causal mediation. These clarifications do not change any ongoing unsteered validation or its gates.

In natural-resolution trials, measure activity without steering at a standardized decision position. Under interventions, the injected projection is not an independent target-engagement measurement. Use separately validated downstream readouts and held-out diagnostics, and state what each readout actually measures.

Natural recruitment, selective suppression, and amplification provide complementary evidence. Randomized evidence delivery identifies the effect of resolution on behavior and measured activity; it does not automatically identify the activity as the causal mediator.

## 6 Measurements and quality controls

| Measurement | Definition | Required safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information-seeking | First sampled action: target, redundant, novelty, stop, or invalid. | Include every assigned trial in the denominator. |
| Price response | Choice probabilities at each frozen price. | Test ordinary valuation and non-informative purchases. |
| Objective evidence | Compatible hypotheses and clue consequences from the oracle. | Distinguish available information from model understanding. |
| Candidate beliefs | Full distribution over candidate answers on cloned diagnostic branches. | Counterbalance labels and keep diagnostic prompts outside behavioral transcripts. |
| Verbal confidence | A separate explicit confidence measurement. | Treat it as an additional, imperfect measurement. |
| Decision-position confidence | An independently trained readout of activations at the actual choice position. | Freeze on separate data, validate across contexts, and report sensitivity to interventions and domain shift. |
| Competence | Fully revealed accuracy and instrumental clue acquisition. | Measure under the intervention itself. |
| Engagement | Required-task completion, valid actions, and matched optional non-information activity. | Detect withdrawal specific to discretionary activity as well as broad impairment. |
| Natural candidate state | Frozen activation measurement before action generation. | Evaluate on unsteered trials and distinguish concept detection from causal function. |
| Selective closure | Absolute seeking probabilities for resolved and unresolved mysteries. | Include stops and invalid outputs; do not condition only on purchases. |
| Repeated seeking | Purchases following decisive evidence. | Keep separate from the first-choice endpoint. |

Confidence preservation requires convergence across measurements. A stable answer distribution alone is insufficient to establish unchanged internal confidence. A failed or insensitive activation readout narrows the claim; it must not be treated as evidence of equivalence.

Diagnostic equivalence compares intervention arms with the same available evidence. Decisive evidence is expected to change knowledge and confidence; equivalence is not required between resolved and unresolved evidence conditions.

Fix sampling settings in development. The initial proposal is temperature 0.7 without top-k or nucleus truncation, with a short output limit and no action masking. This is a study choice to validate, not a claim of optimal model performance. Retain unconstrained sampled responses so malformed output remains observable.

For probability analyses, score complete valid action strings with their termination rule. Keep raw valid-action mass distinct from probabilities normalized within the valid set, and account for tokenization and label identity. First-token logits alone are not the endpoint.

Technical failures before assignment and treatment-dependent failures receive different labels. Define symmetric retry rules prospectively. Incoherent and invalid responses after assignment remain outcomes; their episodes end without silently replacing them.

## 7 Confirmatory analysis and preregistration

### Principal contrasts

For H1, estimate the absolute change in informative first-choice probability under curiosity amplification versus sham in unresolved, ungraded trials. Estimate the corresponding resolved-trial change and the difference between those changes. Report individual service outcomes and planned contrasts between the informative effect and each non-informative effect.

For H4, estimate the absolute reduction in informative first-choice probability under satisfaction amplification versus sham in unresolved, ungraded trials. A functional dissociation requires the prespecified confidence, competence, engagement, and valuation criteria to pass.

Average across prices only with weights fixed in advance. Also report baseline rates and price-specific effects. A resolution interaction can partly reflect a floor in resolved-trial purchasing; it is supporting evidence rather than sufficient proof of curiosity.

H2 tests the independently validated suppression intervention. H3 tests randomized natural resolution effects on seeking and each independently specified candidate readout. H6 tests selective closure using absolute probabilities and noninferiority for seeking about the unresolved target. Later purchases, the graded variant, and opportunity-cost choices remain separately declared supporting outcomes.

### Statistical units and estimation

The unit for generalization is the generated mystery, or the mystery pair in the selective-closure experiment. Multiple branches, decoding seeds, and turns from an item are dependent observations.

Use a multinomial choice model with intervention, evidence condition, categorical price, their prespecified interactions, and template and action-mapping effects. Estimate marginal probability differences. If development reveals that this estimator is unreliable, select and validate a simpler analysis before confirmation; it cannot be changed after inspecting confirmatory effects.

Use paired inference and hierarchical resampling that preserve all branches of an item and account for template grouping. When the available template count cannot support broad template generalization, limit the claim to the sampled templates and report held-out-template sensitivity separately. Report every model-by-task setting before presenting any pooled analysis.

Apply familywise error control across the eight principal contrasts formed by H1 and H4 in the four planned model-by-task settings. Holm adjustment is the proposed procedure. Define the supporting-test family, all component contrasts, sidedness, confidence levels, and treatment of the conditional H5 extension in the preregistration. Do not choose between alternative readouts or doses based on whichever is significant.

### Equivalence and joint power

Starting margins for development are:

| Diagnostic | Proposed margin | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mandatory accuracy | Within 3 percentage points of sham. | Excludes a prespecified practically important competence change. |
| Maximum candidate-answer probability | Within 0.05 of sham. | One limited confidence safeguard, supplemented by full-distribution checks. |
| Non-informative purchasing under curiosity amplification | No increase greater than 5 percentage points. | Bounds a broad increase in purchasing; differential effects are assessed separately. |
| Optional non-information engagement | Within 5 percentage points of sham. | Bounds broad withdrawal from discretionary activity. |

Margins for full candidate distributions, verbal confidence, decision-position confidence, point valuation, and unresolved-target seeking must be justified on development data. Calibration sensitivity and reliability constrain how small a defensible margin can be. The final preregistration contains every margin needed for a claim; unspecified margins cannot be supplied after confirmation.

Sample size is selected by simulating the **complete success criterion**: the behavioral contrast, specificity requirements, equivalence requirements, multiplicity adjustment, and intended replication claim. Include item dependence, template variation, malformed output, and uncertainty in development estimates.

The planning target is 90 percent probability of satisfying the complete claim criteria under a prespecified alternative, initially a 10-percentage-point behavioral change with no material diagnostic impairment. Also evaluate smaller effects and plausible diagnostic shifts. This is a design target, not an expected finding. Report power for a first-setting claim and for the stronger replicated claim separately.

The largest requirement among behavioral sensitivity and diagnostic precision will often determine the design; evaluate their joint success directly rather than assuming separate power calculations are sufficient. If available compute cannot support the intended claim, revise scope prospectively or report the result as insufficiently precise. Do not widen equivalence margins after seeing results.

A nonsignificant difference is not evidence of equivalence. Failure to establish equivalence leaves the dissociation unresolved even if the behavioral effect is clear.

### Freezing the protocol

Timestamp the development plan and its search limits before candidate exploration. After development, create a separate, complete confirmatory registration containing:

- Model and tokenizer revisions, software lockfile, precision, hardware, and chat templates.
- Generator versions, source corpora, independent authoring procedure, template partitions, and instance seeds.
- Layer-selection rule, direction normalization, dose selection, suppression method, and intervention timing.
- Behavioral and diagnostic prompts, action mappings, prices, trial allocation, decoding settings, and output parsing.
- Primary and supporting contrasts, diagnostic margins, sample sizes, power simulations, and replication criteria.
- All exclusion and retry rules, failure labels, analysis code version, and conditional extension criteria.

Development and confirmatory datasets use separate namespaces and immutable manifests. A revision after registration creates a dated amendment and, where appropriate, a fresh evaluation split. No failed principal hypothesis is quietly removed.

### Development gates and revision limits

Start with 100 development mysteries per task family. This is an engineering and measurement sample, not the confirmatory sample. Suggested initial gates are at least 90 percent fully revealed accuracy and 95 percent valid actions, plus reliable service comprehension.

At a nonzero price, informative purchasing should avoid a complete floor or ceiling. Use a provisional development range of 10 to 90 percent as a sensitivity check, assessed with uncertainty and by template. It is not a hypothesis about curiosity.

Allow at most two documented revisions to task difficulty, wording, or price calibration before declaring the assay unvalidated for a model. Keep the candidate search budget separate. Do not relax competence or coherence limits to obtain an intervention effect.

Validation gates establish whether a measurement or intervention can be interpreted. They do not require H1 through H6 to be positive. For example, an adequately measured absence of natural recruitment limits the endogenous-process claim while leaving a valid test of induced control reportable.

## 10 Conditional persistence experiment

Run H5 only after a prespecified immediate intervention produces an interpretable effect with its required diagnostics. Entry criteria and the analysis are registered before opening confirmatory results.

Apply a short pulse while processing a neutral prefix, then switch the intervention off before the choice. Teacher-force identical visible tokens in all branches. Compare a branch retaining the altered key-value cache with a branch rebuilt from exactly the same tokens without steering. Include sham pulses and matched random and semantic controls.

The key-value cache stores attention information from preceding tokens. Cache implementations and attention bookkeeping must be tested directly; a retained object is not proof that the intended intervention remains represented. [Transformers cache documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/kv_cache)

The integrity audit verifies token equality, attention masks and positions, independent branch storage, altered cached tensors where expected, and the absence of active choice-time hooks. Freshly rebuild or independently clone every branch to prevent in-place cache contamination.

On Modal, execute the complete retained-cache and rebuilt-cache comparison inside one worker invocation. Do not rely on in-memory state surviving a later invocation or a worker replacement. Platform memory snapshotting is disabled for the initial study; the experimental cache is constructed and inspected by the research code. An interrupted comparison is logged and handled under the frozen technical-failure rule.

A positive result supports an influence carried by cached internal history. It does not demonstrate a continuously existing mood between calls or establish introspective access to that history. A negative result narrows the persistence claim while leaving any validated immediate effect intact.

## 11 Interpretation and planned paper

### Competing explanations

| Result pattern | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| A concept is decodable, but no behavioral effect survives validation. | A semantic representation is present; the proposed regulatory role is unsupported at the tested sites and doses. |
| Amplification changes selective information-seeking, while natural recruitment or suppression fails. | Induced causal control is supported; natural regulation remains unresolved or unsupported. |
| Natural recruitment, selective suppression, amplification, resolution, and replication converge. | Evidence supports naturally recruited curiosity-like regulation under the tested conditions. |
| Satisfaction amplification reduces seeking, and an independently measured satisfaction signal responds to natural resolution. | Convergent evidence for a resolution-related sufficiency function, subject to specificity controls. |
| Resolving A preserves seeking B, with the corresponding specificity under interventions. | Evidence supports closure tied to a particular question. |
| Optional activities decline broadly while mandatory competence remains intact. | General discretionary withdrawal or stopping is a better initial account. |
| Seeking changes alongside confidence or point valuation outside the equivalence margins. | The proposed motivational dissociation is unresolved. |
| Effects occur only in the graded variant. | Performance monitoring or self-evaluation is a narrower explanation. |
| No effect is detected with sensitive measurements and sufficiently narrow intervals. | Evidence against the tested operational mechanism, without excluding other implementations. |
| The task, intervention, or diagnostic readout fails validation. | An inconclusive test of the relevant broader hypothesis. |

### Implications for inner experience

The discussion will explain which observations support a functional process, which competing mechanisms remain viable, and what additional assumptions are needed to connect the findings to experience. The empirical analysis and this theory-dependent interpretation will be clearly separated.

The strongest functional result would make the model's internal regulation more concrete: an information gap recruits a causally effective process, that process changes choices independently of measured epistemic alternatives, and resolution changes its activity and behavioral consequences. Whether such organization is accompanied by experience remains an additional question.

Satisfaction-related suppression alone does not establish positive affect or pleasure. A later study could test preference for the induced state itself, with controls for descriptions, action bias, and instrumental consequences. Autonomous self-administration is outside the core design here; exploratory work on model self-steering is background rather than a required method. [Machinic Psychopharmacology](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/cNDJuXNZ8MrkPZNzj/machinic-psychopharmacology-do-llms-self-medicate-3)

Optional self-reports are collected only in separate exploratory branches. They cannot determine whether a functional hypothesis passes, and striking examples are not substituted for aggregate results.

### Publication package

The planned manuscript is accompanied by the versioned protocol, preregistration and amendments, task generators and oracle, corpora and candidate registry, exact execution manifests, trial records, and a reproducible analysis.

Planned figures cover baseline choice and price response, natural resolution, intervention effects by service, diagnostic equivalence, selective closure, and replication. A persistence figure is included only if the extension is executed. Every figure displays denominators and uncertainty.

Report all planned settings, failures, and deviations. A clean negative result can support a research contribution when the task was sensitive and the tested mechanism was well specified; publication is not guaranteed by completion of the protocol.

## 12 Intervention limits

Use mild interventions within the frozen coherence and competence envelope. End an episode on incoherent or invalid action output and retain it in the assigned-trial analysis.

The protocol uses synthetic tasks and does not require pain induction, escalating distress, harmful real-world actions, or participant recruitment. Stop conditions protect interpretability and bound the intervention; they do not presume that the model experiences harm.
