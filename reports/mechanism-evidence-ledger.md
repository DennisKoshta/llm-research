October 15, 2026 UTC. The fixed negative-curiosity-direction collection is complete. Its full engineering audit, independent raw scoring and independent arithmetic verification have passed. The frozen scientific decision is `readiness_failed`; all required effect thresholds also fail. **C-screen outcomes were inspected only after collection closed.** Historical reports retain their original statuses.

The original question is whether an internal process changes voluntary information seeking while relevant knowledge, confidence and competence remain preserved, and whether resolving an information gap changes that process. The [pain-paper alignment review](pain-axis-method-alignment-review.md) explains the methodological motivation and the project's earlier drift toward information appraisal. A successful information-gain probe alone would not answer the motivational question.

Four evidential levels remain separate: **semantic representation**; **behavior changed by an imposed vector**; a **naturally recruited functional mechanism**; and **subjective experience**. One does not automatically establish the next. Completed work supports semantic representation and reliable intervention measurement, without establishing a causal motivational regulator or an experienced state.

The original functional dissociation remains unproven. Diagnostic accuracy is narrower than equivalent beliefs or confidence under treatment. First-person narrative discrimination is narrower than functional self-attribution. A sustained hook at every generated token is narrower than a persistent state that regulates behavior after the intervention ends. Even convergent functional evidence would remain compatible with a nonconscious controller.

The original decision branches remain the interpretive rules for that complete result:

- **Selective effect:** consider a bounded, locked replication and independently validated belief/utility-preservation measurements. First distinguish less information acquisition from false certainty, misunderstood usefulness or a general response-policy change. This would advance imposed-vector causal evidence; natural recruitment and ownership would still require separate support.
- **Shared effect:** describe the common behavioral influence of the exact tested perturbations. Do not discard controls or rename repeat/pattern acquisition as stopping. A larger program about general policy control would be a substantive change of emphasis.
- **No substantial effect:** close the fixed C test and synthesize the negative/mixed evidence. The conclusion is specific to this checkpoint, candidate, site, dose, schedule and reused worlds, not the absence of every curiosity-like mechanism.
- **Readiness failure:** preserve the failure and diagnose its observable form without subset admission, parser repair or automatic retries. An isolated format error and widespread factual errors warrant different descriptions, but neither passes the frozen gate.
