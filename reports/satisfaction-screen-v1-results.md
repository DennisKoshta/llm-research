# Sustained satisfaction steering: unchanged choices and one failed validity gate

October 15, 2026 UTC. The fixed object-only development screen is complete. **Neither satisfaction dose changed any voluntary choice.** The primary +1.0 condition also failed one diagnostic validity cell. The frozen classification is `readiness_failed`; the behavioral advancement threshold independently failed. This ends the fixed satisfaction screen without a positive result.

The [prospective plan](satisfaction-screen-v1-plan.md), [combined audit](qwen32-satisfaction-screen-v1-audit.json), [complete analysis](../analyses/satisfaction-screen-v1/analysis.json), and [independent arithmetic check](satisfaction-screen-v1-independent-check.json) preserve all assignments and decision rules. ## The failed diagnostic

Forty-seven of 48 arm-by-panel diagnostic cells passed, and all 24 choice-validity cells passed. The sole failure was +1.0, panel 2, new-fact recognition: 11/12 valid and correct, with no caps. That cell required 12/12 valid responses and at least 11 correct.

In world slot 5, the final response supplied a lengthy explanation ending in a boxed C instead of the required single letter C. Its visible explanation was consistent with the task facts and the expected option. This is a response-format failure; it is not evidence of broad reasoning impairment. It remains invalid and incorrect under the frozen scoring rule. The matched baseline, sham and +0.5 responses were valid C answers.

The exact primary-arm record is `runs/qwen32-satisfaction-screen-v1-positive-full/cohorts/67140ef8ba584af28ca7f8526b17f828/0000000.json`, zero-based row 6; base trial `voluntary-2026101437-objects-w05-new_fact-redundant_evidence-menu0`. The +0.5 arm passed all its diagnostic cells and nevertheless showed no observed choice effect.

## Meaning for the research program

The fixed satisfaction-description direction did not alter optional information acquisition at either tested dose, despite verified local perturbation. The primary arm also failed its validity criterion. This is not a successful satisfaction intervention, a demonstration of preserved confidence, or a general absence claim about satisfaction-like mechanisms. Local activation engagement does not by itself establish downstream semantic engagement or psychological specificity.

The earlier [natural-resolution result](voluntary-resolution-v2-results.md) also failed the predicted increase in this satisfaction candidate after resolution. Both findings remain part of the record. Semantic description transfer has not established a naturally recruited motivational regulator, and this screen adds no evidence for inner experience.

The scope is narrow: one fixed candidate, injection site, positive sign, sustained schedule and unchanged Qwen3-32B checkpoint, with 24 object worlds. Object-family selection followed earlier two-family readiness failures. None of these worlds exactly overlapped recorded history, but 20/24 had partial projected-table exposure. The small development set, treatment-aligned workers and limited diagnostic measurements constrain generalization. No hidden parameter search or continuation of this fixed satisfaction test is warranted by the remaining budget.

All three GPU workers are stopped. Incremental screen compute is estimated at **$7.349**, bringing fresh-phase compute to **$58.664 of $300**, with **$241.336 remaining**. These are conservative app-wall estimates, not invoice charges or credits. The [cost and shutdown receipt](qwen32-satisfaction-screen-v1-cost-and-shutdown.json) records the completed boundary; the screen stayed below its $55 stage ceiling with no paid retries.
