# Native resolution-to-choice contribution: prospective feasibility

October 15, 2026. This is a feasibility assessment, not an executable freeze or admission. No direction has been fitted, no site or donor has been selected, and no new inference has been run for this note. The bounded pain-method control closes first. The scientific target remains a change in voluntary seeking with beliefs, uncertainty judgments and competence preserved. A resolved/unresolved contrast is initially an **epistemic candidate**, not a new definition of curiosity or satisfaction.

## Minimum control-first sequence

1. **Offline nomination, only after declaring it.** Freeze one site, one rank-one estimator, source-row rule and all failure criteria before inspecting new fitted results. A practical development split is all twelve v1 object worlds for extraction and all twelve v2 object worlds for a separate representation check, with one fixed choice-menu copy per delivery. Both sets remain previously exposed development data. Pair decisive with redundant evidence within worlds, and require the proposed resolution representation to generalize across known-property roles and distinguish decisive from independent novelty too. This can reject a weak or literal-delivery feature; passing does not separate knowledge from motivation. No alternate site, sign or fitting search follows failure within this feasibility sequence.

2. **Qualify the missing epistemic readouts before interventions.** Use four assignment-selected v2 object worlds covering both known-property roles. Clone resolved and unresolved records into three readouts, each with two fixed answer orderings: **48 unsteered generations**. Readouts should ask for (a) the complete set of candidates still possible, rather than just the actual answer; (b) a distribution over all four candidates; and (c) whether the particular offered report would eliminate remaining possibilities or merely repeat an already supplied fact. For the noiseless equally likely table, the normative distributions are a singleton after decisive evidence and equal mass on the two compatible candidates after redundant evidence. Retain all outputs, format failures and cap hits. Declare scoring and qualification before running; do not repair wording from these outcomes. Existing factual identification and instructed-useful-action controls remain useful, but cannot substitute for these tests.

   The distributions are elicited reports, not direct access to internal beliefs. Four worlds cannot validate general calibration. Reporting a correct candidate set while shifting probability mass can still reveal a confidence change; a correct final answer alone cannot. The probes must detect the known natural state difference reliably. If they do not, stop the proposed dissociation study rather than silently fall back to easy accuracy.

3. **Only conditionally, one finite causal contribution test.** Existing [StateBackend](../src/curiosity_study/state_backend.py) supports one-position prefill `project` and additive patches followed by fresh reasoning; it does not require a new general execution system. Freeze one centered-component ablation definition and one scalar donor interchange, plus untouched baseline, installed identity sham, a prospectively selected same-state donor and a norm-matched nonspecific perturbation. Bind the donor source row and native scalar value. Match the intervention at the common record token across optional choices and separate epistemic/instructed-action clones. Validate actual BF16 geometry, sham identity and cleanup on the chosen site and physical row layout before scientific generation. Preserve real selected-action consequences using the existing receipt path.

   Centered ablation is not projection onto an intrinsically meaningful zero. A donor scalar inside its observed range does not guarantee the recipient's complete activation remains on the native distribution. Do not transplant a resolved full residual vector and interpret the result as motivation. A compact downstream schedule could use the twelve v2 worlds, both evidence states, two optional menus plus three diagnostic branches, and the six conditions above: 720 generations, plus 72 existing instructed-useful-action clones on the unresolved records, or **792 generations**. This is a sizing example, not an assigned schedule; readout qualification may show it is scientifically inadequate, and a measured forecast and explicit later freeze remain necessary.

## Decisive interpretation limits

The epistemic and incremental-report measurements must be primary competing explanations, not permissive side gates. If the intervention changes possible-candidate sets, confidence allocations or report usefulness, a seeking change supports altered epistemic routing; it does not satisfy the original knowledge–motivation dissociation. Preserving only easy factual answers warrants no stronger claim. Small samples and insensitive or saturated probes cannot establish equivalence or noninferiority.

Even preserved cloned readouts require caution: their independent reasoning can recover facts from the intact prompt after a lesion. They do not prove that appraisal was unchanged at the precise moment of the optional decision. A record-token intervention also leaves other prompt positions and their cache representations intact, so a null cannot establish that native resolution information is unnecessary everywhere. The supportable first positive claim is a **native contribution to evidence-sensitive optional choice**, qualified by the measured epistemic preservation and nonspecific controls—not satisfaction, curiosity, calibrated confidence preservation or experience.

The current captures make that narrower experiment technically feasible. They do not yet justify paying for the causal stage under a stronger motivational claim. Offline nomination and the bounded readout qualification have a concrete stop decision; inadequate evidence is a useful outcome, not a reason to expand an information-appraisal program or spend the remaining allowance.

## Prospective boundary clarified before qualification

October 15, 2026. The [48-generation qualification plan](epistemic-readout-qualification-v1-plan.md) is authorized for preparation, without a fitted native direction. Even a full pass **does not admit the 792-generation sizing example above**. A native motivational test would still require an independently validated passive epistemic readout on the actual treated choice trajectory, or a validated shared-state short-readout fork that avoids fresh long diagnostic reasoning. Accurate separately generated answers can recover knowledge from the intact record after an intervention. If this stronger measurement is unavailable or insensitive, the proposed resolution contrast cannot support the original belief-preserved motivational claim; generic evidence-to-choice routing is not a substitute.
