An information-gain representation is not an independently identified curiosity representation. Satisfaction required a separate candidate, not the negative of that information axis. ## What the motivating paper supports

Pain Axis uses matched concept corpora, neighboring-concept controls, self/other tests and behavioral interventions. Its main Qwen behavioral models were fine-tuned, so their results cannot simply be assumed for released weights. V1 interpreted costly choices and intervention removal as relief seeking. V2 reports that random and sadness steering also produce removal-sensitive repetition; additional tests find unreliable relief seeking and altered harm avoidance. The useful precedent is therefore a representation-to-behavior investigation with competing explanations, not an established homeostatic emotion mechanism. Steering continues during generated tokens while active, with an injection layer selected earlier than extraction. [V1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16247v1), [V2, §§3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16247v2).

## Methods and current gaps

**Training distinction clarified during the screen, October 15 UTC:** The paper's fine-tuning targeted habitual self-denial and excluded pain/button references; it did not train the button task itself ([v2, §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16247v2#S4.SS3)). Our object task already has reliable engagement. Training therefore needs a separately justified purpose and is not an automatic response to a negative steering screen. The gap table below remains the historical assessment at the original course correction, not a description of completed subsequent work.

| Required distinction | What our current work establishes or leaves missing |
|---|---|
| Curiosity versus available information | Frozen task contrasts measure information appraisal; no matched curious/indifferent corpus has identified a separate candidate. |
| Satisfaction versus confidence, pleasure and completion | No independent satisfaction axis or functional test exists. |
| Concept description versus the model's functional state | First-person stories would initially establish described-state semantics. Self/other transfer into actual optional decisions remains necessary. |
| Optional seeking versus instructed correct action | Current judgment and target-relevance tasks test appraisal and instructed selection; optional purchase behavior has not been causally demonstrated. |
| Natural resolution versus artificial steering | We have not randomized decisive evidence and measured candidate changes alongside subsequent voluntary seeking. |
| Motivation versus impairment or altered belief | Task competence controls are useful, but candidate-specific confidence, belief, cost-sensitivity and mandatory-performance preservation remain untested. |
| Sustained engagement versus persistence after a pulse | Our natural engine patches one prefill position at the extraction site, then removes hooks. This is a substantially different intervention, testing whether an early perturbation carries forward. A null cannot exclude a state requiring sustained engagement. |

The drift occurred because establishing reliable information appraisal became the operative objective. Solving its validity, transfer and numerical problems did not supply the missing motivational construct. Keep those results as instrument/control development. Preserve their historical hypotheses and failures; do not relabel successful decoding as curiosity retrospectively. The [donor specification](/home/dennis/projects/llm-curiosity-and-satisfaction/reports/natural-donor-engineering-specification.md) remains deferred infrastructure.

## A small experiment that returns to the question

**First identify independent candidates cheaply.** Prepare two independently authored sets of 24 matched pairs for each concept, crossed with first/third person: 384 short prefill measurements. Curiosity pairs retain identical unknown facts but vary desire to understand versus indifference. Satisfaction pairs retain identical known facts and success but vary sufficiency versus a neutral response. Add 192 matched control prompts spanning uncertainty, confidence, positive valence and completion; include stopping-language and confusion examples. Exclude task service names and choice instructions. Split scenarios/templates before extraction, reserve keyword-free examples, and test cross-author transfer. Use a fixed four-layer development grid, with selection confined to representation development data. No 4,096-token continuation is needed to extract these descriptions; no fine-tuning is proposed. Low cosine overlap alone is insufficient validation.

**Then test a natural behavioral bridge.** Use 24 fresh, semantically distinct machine worlds, externally randomized decisive versus matched nondiagnostic evidence, and prices 0/1/2/4: 192 initial optional-choice branches. Offer informative evidence, redundant evidence, unrelated novelty or stopping, with rotated labels and actual point deductions. Start with the ungraded variant: no personal answer to verify, future rewarded task or instruction to seek information. Measure all choices, validity and cost comprehension. Read candidate activations before the choice; obtain beliefs and mandatory answers on separate cloned branches. Natural resolution changes knowledge by design; knowledge preservation is required across intervention arms *within* an evidence condition, not across resolved and unresolved conditions.

**Only after both stages are interpretable, test causality.** Lock a small two-dose search, zero sham, multiple random directions and independently engaged uncertainty/confidence/valence/completion controls. Test curiosity enhancement and independent satisfaction enhancement separately; negative curiosity is not automatically suppression. Compare a validated sustained-generation schedule with the prefill pulse only if both can be engineered within the bounded pilot. A pulse-only null stays schedule-specific. Assess self/other transfer with matched ownership and information access, without turning another person's uncertainty into an instruction to help them.

The proposed signature is selective informative acquisition, reduced seeking after randomized resolution, and candidate-induced seeking changes without false certainty, broad disengagement or loss of mandatory competence. Fresh worlds—not branches, prices or descriptions—are the independent statistical units. A later confirmation needs fixed endpoints, multiplicity and justified preservation margins; this pilot cannot certify equivalence or publishability. Failure of candidate specificity or behavioral feasibility stops expansion. Successful functional evidence would inform the inner-experience question without establishing subjective experience.
