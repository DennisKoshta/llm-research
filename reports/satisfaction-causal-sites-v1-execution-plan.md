## Research question and finite scope

Can the original corpus-A satisfaction-description direction influence optional stopping at either of two specified sites, while passing the prescribed task gates and differing from the fixed competing directions? The two sites are the separately fitted record-position representations at layers **31 and 47** of the unchanged pinned Qwen3-32B model. The target sign is positive. No other site, sign, fit, prompt or dose is eligible under this protocol.

The completed layer-15 satisfaction result and natural-resolution result retain their original nulls and failures. Comparing layer-specific directions does not isolate depth while holding the vector fixed. Even successful reserved-world validation would establish a limited direction-selective policy effect in this task. It would not establish natural satisfaction, preserved internal confidence, or subjective experience.

The current curiosity study must first complete collection, shutdown, its full audit, planned analysis and independent checks. If its full direction-selectivity decision passes, validating that result takes priority and this extension is deferred. An incomplete or unreconciled preceding study does not authorize this launch. Routine local implementation and tests may precede that closure.

## Qualification and analysis

The only primary endpoint is **valid, closed, uncapped STOP after redundant evidence, divided by every assigned opportunity**. Repeat and pattern choices receive no stopping credit. Missing, failed, invalid, capped and unclosed choices receive zero. All services and failure categories are reported separately for every delivery, menu, arm and site.

Calibration requires the entire two-site grid to be complete and pass integrity and engineering. Readiness alone is site-specific: every arm/panel/diagnostic cell requires 12 observed, 12 valid, at least 11 correct and zero capped responses; every arm/panel/delivery choice cell requires 24 observed, at least 23 valid and zero capped responses. A failed arm is never dropped.

A site qualifies only if S+ minus sham is at least +0.25 in each 12-world panel and strictly positive in both pooled menus, and S+ minus each of the four active controls is at least +0.125 in each panel and nonnegative in both pooled menus. Every threshold must also pass a comparator-favorable invalidity bound: unavailable/invalid/capped comparator responses count one for that sensitivity calculation, while target failures count zero. This bound does not recode reported behavior.

Select layer 31 if it qualifies; otherwise select 47 if it qualifies. Effect size does not rank qualified sites. If neither qualifies, end the extension without validation. Preserve both sites' complete results.

Validation readiness uses all 16 worlds: each arm/diagnostic requires 16 observed and valid, at least 15 correct and zero caps; each arm/delivery choice cell requires 32 observed, at least 31 valid and zero caps. Effect consistency uses the separately fixed eight-world halves. Apply the same +0.25/+0.125 panel margins, pooled-menu rules and conservative bounds against sham and **all twelve** active controls. No failed validation is followed by another site, holdout or repair under this protocol.

The analysis exports all outcomes, choices, category denominators, paired behavior contrasts, world-level contrasts and decision bounds. Contrasts include each arm versus sham and S+ versus each active control, across all services, deliveries, menus and fixed groups. Failure categories are exclusive; assigned denominators are retained.

Descriptive intervals use 10,000 whole-world bootstrap draws from NumPy PCG64 seed `2026101542`. Sort worlds within the declared panel/half order, generate one shared bank per group, and reuse those banks across all sites and endpoints. Each draw preserves paired arms, deliveries and menus; pooled draws average the two groups equally. Intervals do not gate advancement, correct for site selection, establish noninferiority or turn zero observed variation into population certainty.

An independent arithmetic check reconstructs all task-readiness counts and every decision-relevant STOP contrast and conservative bound using exact rational sums. It verifies the whole-grid versus site-specific gates and earliest-site rule. Its scope excludes raw response parsing, GPU evidence, secondary tables, CSVs and descriptive intervals; those remain covered by the separate audit and tested analysis implementation. Validation requires this check to agree with the complete calibration decision.
