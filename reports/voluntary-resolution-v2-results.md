## Implications and next bounded question

This revision grants no causal admission. It closes further wording repair of the machine instrument. The object family passed every unchanged diagnostic in both v1 and v2, supporting a separately declared object-only exploratory experiment. Choosing that family after these results is a disclosed development decision; it does not turn the failed two-family collection into a success or establish cross-family generalization.

The next useful question is whether sustained positive steering along the fixed satisfaction-description direction can increase voluntary stopping on unresolved object trials. That is a sufficiency test of an imposed perturbation. Because natural satisfaction transfer failed, even a positive result would not establish natural satisfaction mediation. Preserved measured diagnostics, semantic and random controls, and additional belief measurements would be needed before a stronger motivational interpretation. No claim of inner experience follows from these data.

Actual-model short and full-cap engineering checks must precede a separately frozen scientific plan. The proposed screen will keep the fixed site/sign and two doses, retain every assigned outcome, and stop this fixed test for null or impaired results. It will not initiate a layer, sign or dose search.
