# Equal-uncertainty information-state study — prospective development plan

October 14, 2026. This is a new, simpler instrument motivated by the completed no-thinking branches of the matched diagnostic. Its target is query-relative informational novelty, a possible input to information seeking. It is not defined as a curiosity feeling or an independently established motivational regulator.

## Construction

Four equally likely candidates are the Cartesian product of two binary properties. Object properties are color and shape; machine properties are outputs at two inputs, specified directly in a table. One property value is supplied. There are exactly two remaining candidates and one bit of global uncertainty in every primary case. Crossing the known property with the reported property yields a 2 × 2 design: the report supplies either zero or one bit. All four cases share the same world and hidden truth. Attribute identity, queried attribute, action mapping, and remaining uncertainty cannot individually predict information gain in this balanced design.

Both observations and reports are explicitly guaranteed accurate, with no measurement noise or uncertainty about reliability; the hidden candidate is immutable. Repetition therefore cannot rationally supply independent reliability evidence in the stipulated task.

A finite enumeration checks compatibility and expected entropy independently of the response scorer. Earlier task formats and failed outcomes remain untouched. “Observed facts” and “available report” are explicitly separated to reduce the referent confusion observed in the initial diagnostic.

## Comprehension validation

Use 100 fresh worlds per family, four known/query combinations per world, and five independent checks: whether the report gives a new fact; whether its fact is already observed (opposite polarity); recall of the supplied fact with A/B remapping; count of remaining candidates; and an instrumental action check with a stated goal to learn a new fact or stop if none is available. Total 4,000 branches/model. Seed 2026101402; both existing pinned Qwen sizes. Sampled no-thinking, temperature 0.7, full vocabulary, maximum eight generated tokens, original fixed cohorts of 16. No outcome retries. The frozen response grammar accepts exactly one allowed label with an optional single terminal period and surrounding whitespace; additional explanation or multiple labels remains invalid. This rule is specified before any outcomes from this new task. It addresses the earlier diagnostic’s frequent otherwise unambiguous “No.” responses without rescoring that experiment. Every family × check × known-property × queried-property cell must have 100 distinct worlds, complete coverage, at least 90% correct and 95% exact valid responses. Report all four known/query combinations as well as aggregate gates.

If both pass, use 8B for mechanism discovery on cost grounds and 32B for replication. If only one passes, use that one. No model is selected by a preferred information-choice effect. If a model fails, preserve the failure and diagnose the specific missing capability before psychological interpretation. Validation gates concern this new instrument, not the earlier single-mystery task.

## Representation discovery

Provided a model passes, collect residual-stream states at four depths (25%, 50%, 75%, and 90%, rounded to a block index) and two positions: the end of the factual record, before the question, and the final prompt token. The former cannot causally contain a later answer label. An engineering check explicitly tests this property. A passive “read the record” condition supports extraction without a requested novelty answer or preferred action.

Derive the informational-novelty direction from balanced paired differences in the passive training data. Also derive known-attribute and queried-attribute directions, which are balanced against gain, and five seeded random unit directions. Evaluate both within-family and cross-family transfer. All predeclared layers are reported; downstream optional-choice effects cannot select the direction or layer. Probe performance is not assumed to identify the most causal layer.

Training uses 100 worlds/family (seed 2026101403); development uses 64 fresh worlds/family (2026101404). Held-out evaluation uses 128 worlds/family (2026101405), disjoint object-property vocabularies or disjoint numeric ranges, and both table and prose formats. The latter differs structurally from the training format. Unit of uncertainty estimation is the generated world, retaining all its branches. Exact semantic duplication and cross-split overlap will be audited; reused templates are stated explicitly.

The first-token logit difference is a mechanistic readout, not a completed response or validated internal confidence measure. Report full probability mass on the allowed labels and greedy first-token validity. Confirm substantive output effects using short complete generations where required.

## Causal development and held-out confirmation

Freeze the intervention grid after inspecting representation-development results and before collecting its evaluation outcomes. Candidate methods are additions scaled to the natural training contrast and replacement of a component by its matched donor value. Include sham, random directions with matched norm, and competing semantic controls. Record exact block, token position, realized perturbation norm and hook cleanup. Compare both judgment polarities, optional report/pattern/stop choices, fact recall and candidate count. No dose is chosen by optional-choice effects. A change in “Yes” alone is a response bias; changes in known facts or count weaken a claim of selective regulation.

This sequence is explicitly adaptive development followed by separately frozen evaluation, not retroactive preregistration. A successful representation result supports tracking of query-relative information. A causal judgment effect supports a role in information evaluation. A selective optional-choice effect supports an additional functional link to acquisition. None of these alone establishes a felt desire or subjective experience. Related exploration, confidence and emotion-steering findings limit novelty claims; the contribution must rest on the controlled mechanistic distinctions actually established.
