# Planned interpretation of the one-credit calibration

Prepared October 7, 2026, before the complete batched choice sample exists. This note makes the planned readout explicit; it adds no advancement gate and changes no assignments or scores.

## First establish what was measured

Report exact assigned and completed coverage, execution failures, invalid answers, and every frozen validity/frequency/layout criterion. The incomplete serial calibration remains separate. Neither its 82 responses nor the cloud smoke belongs in the new calibration's denominators. The full batch calibration, if admitted, contains 100 task pairs per family, three evidence conditions, and two layouts: 1,200 branches.

Each assignment starts with one clue credit. X and Y are mutually exclusive options; stopping retains the credit but has no separately valued alternative use. This is a choice between information sources, not monetary willingness to pay or a test of intrinsic reward. The chosen clue is delivered, but no later use of that clue is observed in this single-choice assay.

## Primary descriptive readout

For each family and layout, show X, Y, stop, invalid, and technical-failure counts and probabilities using all assigned branches. Then pool layouts within family and show the three evidence conditions. A separate confidence interval analysis resamples entire task pairs, retaining all six related branches together (5,000 bootstrap repetitions, fixed seed 20261008). These are pointwise descriptive intervals; they do not authorize a confirmatory claim or generalize beyond the sampled task construction.

Show all resolution differences relative to both-unresolved, including each target's absolute selection probability and stopping. If resolving X increases Y's share only among successful information choices, that is insufficient to say Y seeking was preserved or increased. Use Y's absolute probability, keeping invalids and stops in the denominator. The same applies with X and Y exchanged. No noninferiority margin has been fixed, so the report cannot declare preservation via a non-significant decrease.

## Headroom and limitations

For both-unresolved trials, information-choice probability is p(X) + p(Y). The observed algebraic room for an upward effect is 1 minus that probability; if invalid and failure rates remain fixed, it is p(stop). The maximum downward change equals current information-choice probability. Report both layouts and pooled values. These are sample-based bounds, not power calculations or additional pass/fail criteria.

A comprehension pass with poor choice validity still leaves the instrument unusable under its frozen calibration rule. Strong layout sensitivity limits interpretation even when raw information seeking is frequent. A ceiling in information choices limits curiosity-amplification tests; suppression at that ceiling may still be measurable but could reflect disengagement or impaired competence. A baseline resolution effect is not a required gate and does not establish satisfaction or endogenous regulation.

This assay lacks an independent non-information activity, an independently valued cost, the full diagnostic battery, and validated curiosity/satisfaction directions. Therefore even a calibrated instrument supports only the next development stage. Representation extraction must use independent corpora and controls, with no direction selected for producing a desired choice effect. Any transition to a narrower paper claim or broader instrument redesign is a substantive research decision, to be presented explicitly rather than disguised as a routine parameter adjustment.
