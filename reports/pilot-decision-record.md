## Scope decision pending

The recommended next direction is a narrower first study of voluntary information seeking about one question, with a separate non-information alternative and completion diagnostics. The stronger selective-satisfaction claim—resolving one question while preserving interest in another—would be deferred. The alternative retains both curiosity and selective satisfaction as equal priorities and redesigns the broader environment before representation work. This changes the first study's scientific scope, so it has been brought to the sponsor as the high-level decision requested in the autonomy instruction. No new assay or spending has been launched pending that direction.

## What the evidence changes

The failures make interface validation central to this project. A missing or unsuitable answer cannot be read as absence of curiosity. Conversely, correct formatting cannot substitute for understanding. Short-output and thinking settings introduce different measurement limitations, and successful diagnosis of one limitation does not validate the whole assay.

The comprehension pass permitted a test of discretionary choices; that calibration failed. Even a later successful calibration would leave independent concept construction, target engagement, confidence and engagement controls, natural recruitment, replication, and confirmatory registration unresolved. Existing work already demonstrates exploration steering and emotion-related behavioral effects; our eventual contribution must be more specific than showing that a vector changes choices. [Targeted literature notes](literature-check.md)

The next major scientific decision arises if the bounded development work cannot provide an instrument for the intended contrasts and proceeding would require broader redesign or changing the central question. Residual uncertainty about subjective experience is an expected limitation of the project, not by itself a reason to stop routine work or request approval. Failed thresholds must remain recorded.
