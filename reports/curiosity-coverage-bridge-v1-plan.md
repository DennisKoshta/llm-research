## Inferential boundary

A pass would establish schedule-dependent **imposed-vector perturbability of optional policy** relative to this fixed control panel on exposed development tasks. Full coverage may change instruction interpretation, task appraisal or response construction. Equal norm and numerical engagement do not establish functional engagement of neighboring psychological constructs.

The 48 unsteered readout checks, even if passed, qualify independently elicited long-reasoning reports. They do not establish preserved beliefs on a treated choice trajectory: fresh reasoning can reconstruct information from the intact prompt. Any claim of motivation dissociated from knowledge still needs sensitive contemporaneous epistemic measurement, native recruitment and causal contribution/necessity, plus genuinely new materials for confirmation. No successful bridge establishes experienced curiosity, satisfaction or consciousness. No native-component fitting, causal panel or further pain-method calibration is admitted here.
