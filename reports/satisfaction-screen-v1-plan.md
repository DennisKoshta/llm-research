# Fixed object-only sustained satisfaction screen, v1

Prospective development plan, 2026-10-15 UTC. Written after the complete voluntary-resolution v2 results and before selection of these worlds, tokenization, or any model output from this screen. Execution requires a passing, independently reproduced full-cap sustained-engineering audit. This plan does not itself grant that admission.

The question is whether the independently selected satisfaction-description direction can causally increase valid voluntary stopping while the model continues to pass the assigned object-task checks. This follows the original pain paper's use of sustained internal steering and behavioral tests. Our model has unchanged weights; the pain paper's main behavioral model was trained. This experiment is therefore an exploratory adaptation, not a replication or a test that identifies subjective satisfaction.

The preceding two-family assay failed its global readiness criterion. Objects passed all six diagnostic cells in both versions; machines did not. Selecting objects now is a disclosed development decision made after those results. The natural satisfaction projection also failed the predicted increase after resolution. A future steering effect would establish an effect of an imposed vector, not natural mediation of resolution, an endogenous need, or inner experience. Neither previous failure is removed from the record.

## Fixed analysis and advancement decision

The sole primary arm is +1.0 versus zero sham; +0.5 is a reported sensitivity condition and cannot rescue it. The primary endpoint is the fraction of all assigned redundant-delivery choices that validly stop with a closed, uncapped response. Average the two menus within each world, then the twelve worlds within each panel. Give panels equal weight in pooled summaries. Invalidity, caps, and failures never count as stopping. Report every service and failure category in all deliveries and arms, every world contrast, all diagnostic cells, and all denominators.

A positive development screen requires an increase of at least 0.25 in **each** panel's primary endpoint, strictly positive pooled differences for **both** action menus, and every gate above. This requires at least six net additional valid stops among the 24 redundant choices per panel. It is a deliberately substantial development threshold, not a power calculation, significance test, or psychologically calibrated minimum. Report weaker, opposite, and null effects without changing the threshold. A sensitivity-only success does not advance.

Use 10,000 whole-world bootstrap draws with seed2026101438. Sort panels panel1 then panel2, and semantic-world IDs within each. Draw twelve worlds independently within each panel, keeping every arm, delivery, menu, and diagnostic branch of each sampled world together. Reuse the same draw banks for every endpoint. Report descriptive percentile95% intervals; interval exclusion or p-values do not determine advancement. This small finite development sample does not support population-wide certainty. There is no new representation fitting or projection-based selection.

If this fixed screen passes, the next separate prospective experiment must test specificity against already constructed neighboring concept directions and matched perturbations, together with unresolved-case knowledge/confidence and instrumental behavior checks. These are needed to distinguish stopping from generic completion, confidence, valence, compliance, or impairment. A positive screen alone is not paper-worthy evidence of satisfaction or knowledge–motivation dissociation. If it fails, end this fixed site/sign/dose screen and report the result; do not conduct an unannounced search over layers, signs, wording, or alternative curiosity vectors.
