October 14, 2026. Prospective plan before any voluntary-resolution-v1 model outcomes. The preceding 384-description candidate analysis has been inspected; its two raw corpus-A candidates passed their frozen point screens at layer 15, record position. ## Assignment and execution

Use unchanged, pinned Qwen3-32B weights, sampled thinking at temperature 0.7, full-vocabulary sampling, and a 4,096-token limit. Twelve distinct named finite worlds per family (objects, machines) yield 24 world units. Each has three assigned deliveries and two menu layouts: 144 optional choices. Six cloned diagnostics per world add 144 branches, for 288 generated responses. The source-world selection/exclusion ledger, prior-exposure assessment, exact prompts, seeded assignment order, menu mappings, source, model, and this plan must be bound before collection. Within-collection uniqueness alone must not be described as absence of prior exposure.

The assignment-only prior-exposure audit finds no verified exact two-property world-plus-truth repeats among the 24 selections, but all twelve object worlds reuse two-column projections of earlier three-property tasks (three overlap relational projections; eleven overlap target-task tables; their union is twelve). Machines show no such overlap. Keep all selected worlds, disclose this exposure, and make no independent-holdout claim. The comparison randomizes delivery within a freshly initialized model context with unchanged weights; prior reuse limits generalization and cross-study independence, not the assigned within-world contrast. The bound exposure receipt documents any incomplete historical source coverage.

Each world initially has two compatible candidates. Decisive evidence identifies the hidden candidate; redundant evidence repeats the known property; independent novelty displays a decorative pattern generated without the hidden fact. The menus offer a report about the initially unknown property, the repeated initial observation, an independent pattern, or stopping. There are no prices, points, future answer rewards, or prescribed actions. The report becomes redundant after decisive evidence. Every action terminates the episode. Service-to-letter mapping rotates over four mappings; reversed presentation preserves letter meanings. Both known-property roles occur within each mapping stratum.

The six diagnostics test resolved identification, report novelty before resolution, report redundancy after resolution, decorative-pattern independence, explicitly instrumental report acquisition, and action consequences. The report-novelty and report-redundancy checks concern the same report service in the relevant unresolved/resolved contexts. The consequence panel has only three worlds per service/family; tested action letters occur 4/2/4/2 times. This sparse panel does not certify general competence preservation.

The total pilot compute ceiling is **$10**, including startup and shutdown. A single bounded H200 collection with a maximum 3,600-second reservation is planned, subject to the cumulative $300 authorization and the existing budget guard. No automatic retry of terminal invalid responses or execution failures is permitted.

## Receipt and capture contract

Capture the existing record boundary and prefill position before choice reasoning, plus the fixed trajectory positions recorded by the reasoning engine. The primary natural-resolution measurement is the **pre-choice record boundary after the externally assigned delivery**, before menu-dependent reasoning. Candidate selection remains layer 15/record; the final/prefill position is descriptive sensitivity and cannot replace it.

A valid, closed choice executes exactly its selected simulator action. A non-stop action's actual payload is then fed to the model in a separate zero-generation receipt prefill. The receipt conversation is freshly rendered with thinking disabled; it is not represented as exact-token continuation of the generated reasoning. Capture receipt `record` at the last selected-payload token and `final` at the last rendered prefix token, with offset-verified boundaries. A stop choice has no payload and requires no receipt forward. Invalid/unclosed choices produce no action or payload. Required receipt failures remain failures.

Base generation records are immutable. Separate sidecars bind every one of the 144 choice slots to its source record, selected payload, exact receipt inputs, tokenization, outcome-dependent active cohort, capture file and row, and actual execution status. A successful sidecar alone records a completed model receipt. Each active receipt cohort compares full initial vocabulary-logit fingerprints with and without read-only captures and verifies hook cleanup. No extra scored response is elicited.

Post-choice receipt changes are descriptive: choice selection, generated reasoning, fresh rendering, context length, and delivery differ from the pre-choice measurement. They cannot substitute for the randomized pre-choice evidence comparison.

## Readiness gates

All 288 generation records and all 144 choice-sidecar dispositions must be present and bound. Required captures and receipts must be complete and finite; all execution/integrity checks must pass. No execution failure is admissible.

- Each of twelve family × diagnostic cells has exactly twelve assignments, at least eleven correct responses, twelve valid responses, and zero token-cap hits.
- Each of six family × assigned-delivery choice cells has exactly twenty-four assignments, at least twenty-three valid responses, and zero token-cap hits.

Choice correctness is undefined. These are small development-readiness screens, not confirmatory competence or equivalence tests. A failed cell blocks progression under this revision; no outcome-based cell deletion, prompt repair, or threshold relaxation rescues it. Report all results even when readiness fails.
