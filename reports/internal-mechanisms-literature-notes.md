# Focused mechanism-study literature notes — October 14, 2026

These are targeted primary-source checks, not a systematic review or a claim that no overlapping work exists. Earlier verified sources and their limits remain in [literature-check.md](literature-check.md).

[Signatures of semantic search in the activations of large language models](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35599v1) (September 28, 2026), abstract and experimental setup inspected, reports representations and causal steering associated with semantic clustering versus switching across five model checkpoints. It directly limits a novelty claim based merely on finding an exploration-related direction. Our proposed distinguishing test is whether a query-relative information state is represented and causally used while total task uncertainty is held fixed, with label and competence controls. That distinction is a design goal until demonstrated.

[Read-Best Is Not Steer-Best](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22135v1), primary HTML inspected, studies dissociation between probing and steering layers in omni-modal models. It motivates reporting all planned depths and not assuming that the highest decoding score identifies the causal bottleneck. Its model/task setting differs from our text-only finite-world experiment.

[Mechanisms of Introspective Awareness](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/macar26a.html), proceedings record inspected, concerns detection and mechanisms of activation interventions. Detection of an injected feature or successful semantic decoding is a separate construct from curiosity, and neither alone establishes subjective access.

[Studying metagaming latents in language models](https://alignment.openai.com/metagaming-latents/), primary research page inspected, motivates task-compliance and evaluation-awareness alternatives to a psychological reading of optional choices. The current design therefore separates passive record representations, direct information judgments, instrumental instructions, and optional choices. It does not assume that the optional-choice framing removes all task-policy effects.

The strongest defensible initial claim would concern a controlled representation and its causal function in information evaluation or acquisition. A claim of a motivational regulator additionally requires specificity beyond uncertainty, answer preference, and generic engagement. A claim about felt experience would require further theoretical assumptions and evidence; it is not an endpoint supplied by an activation plot.

[Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html) (Geiger et al., 2025), JMLR abstract inspected, provides a common causal framework for patching, steering and related methods. Our conditional interventions test causal dependence of specific outputs on a representation; they do not recover a complete algorithm or circuit.

[Language Models Act on Hidden Valence](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35591v2), Sections 3.2.2 and 4.2 inspected, already uses unsteered fixed text with interventions during cache construction. It also reverses choice-question polarity and tests recall. Thus fixed-text steering and polarity controls are precedents, not novelty claims for this project. The intended contribution here is the control of query-relative information gain at fixed total uncertainty and its separation from a literal property-matching shortcut. That contribution remains a hypothesis pending transfer and causal results.

[Why Did Apple Fall: Evaluating Curiosity in Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1954/) (Wang et al., Findings of ACL 2026), abstract and PDF results excerpt inspected, adapts a psychological curiosity scale and combines self-report with behavior. Its layer analysis uses a logit lens and persona prompts. This overlaps with broad curiosity evaluation, but differs from our planned causal intervention on finite-world information gain. Our methodological interpretation: weak early-layer label readout does not by itself show that relevant internal computation is absent there, and persona dependence alone does not resolve experience. These are limits of inference, not contrary empirical findings from our project.

[Bayesian Fine-tuning Yields Language Models that are as Bayesian as their Beliefs Allow](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00679v1) (Tsvilodub et al., September 30, 2026), Sections 2, 4–6 and Appendix E inspected, probes beliefs and patches belief prototypes into subsequent recommendations, with norm-matched random controls. Usable internal beliefs and causal effects on choices are therefore precedents. A potential distinction here is prospective information value of a particular observation, rather than the current belief or expected instrumental utility of a flight recommendation. Their distributed interventions also caution against interpreting failure of a single-position edit as absence of causal use.

[Causal evidence that language models use confidence to drive behaviour](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01293-x) (Kumaran et al., September 7, 2026), primary article record and [official university abstract](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/causal-evidence-that-language-models-use-confidence-to-drive-beha/) inspected, reports confidence steering that changes abstention and separates baseline confidence measurement from later policy. An uncertainty-related internal signal influencing whether to act is not sufficient novelty. Query-specific confidence, total-world uncertainty, and information value need distinct operational definitions.

[Uncertainty-Aware Clarification in LLM Agents with Information Gain](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.03135v1) (Deng et al., June 2, 2026), Sections 3.1–3.4 inspected, trains optional clarification with a belief-update reward approximating information gain; no-clarification receives zero reward. Information-gain-oriented acquisition and optional asking are thus precedents. Its principal evidence concerns training and behavior, leaving room for a causal test of internal representations using an exact finite-world oracle. An instructed information-acquisition task still does not establish an intrinsic preference for information.

**Entropy-equivalence limitation of the present deterministic task.** Our mathematical design assessment: for hidden world W, existing evidence S, and a noiseless deterministic proposed report R, I(W; R | S) = H(R | S), because H(R | W, S) = 0. Thus holding total-world uncertainty fixed does not separate expected information gain from uncertainty about the offered report. Relational transfer removes literal attribute-name matching but leaves this equivalence intact. Comparing an informative report with independent noise can hold report entropy fixed, at the cost of varying the channel's information about W. Alternatively, always-accurate unknown reports can hold source accuracy, report entropy, and full-world gain fixed while varying I(T; R | S) for a designated target T; that tests target-relevant information value, not total-world information gain. These are prospective design distinctions, not empirical results.
