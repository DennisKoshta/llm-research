## Natural continuation: engineering passed

The separate 416-continuation collection used 32 distinct source prefixes and thirteen conditions. All records passed integrity checks, including exact baseline/sham generated-token, initial-logit, probability, and initial-capture identity, finite interventions, correct source bindings, and hook cleanup. The longest baseline generated 880 tokens. Every baseline answered correctly (32/32).

The [engineering audit](../analyses/relational-natural-v2-engineering/analysis.json) reports `engineering_passed: true` and `eligible_for_interpretation: false`. This is a small engineering sample without frozen behavioral-readiness criteria; intervention-effect estimates are not promoted to scientific evidence. Passing this test does not repair the earlier fixed-transcript replay failures, and the large appraisal confirmation remains deferred.

During local auditing, vocabulary-size lookup was hoisted out of the per-token loop. The tokenizer is immutable; the token range, decoding, EOS, source, and parity checks are unchanged. No cloud generation or frozen threshold changed.
