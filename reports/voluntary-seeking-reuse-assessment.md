# Returning to voluntary seeking and resolution

October 14, 2026. **Recommendation: run one small, unsteered behavioral feasibility pilot before expanding mechanism work.** Its question is whether the model voluntarily acquires a particular unknown fact and changes that choice after the fact is supplied. Successful information judgments are useful controls; they do not establish a desire to know. This follows the [original protocol](/home/dennis/.codex/attachments/6906d991-e6d1-4d5b-a952-c186af038789/llm-curiosity-and-satisfaction-experimental-design.md), which targets a dissociation between knowledge and motivation, plus a response to resolution. This assessment proposes no frozen schedule, new dataset, fine-tuning, or launch.

## Reuse, with the right labels

Reuse the explicit finite tables, truthful noiseless reports, and oracle from [information_state.py](../src/curiosity_study/information_state.py); its `new`, `repeat`, and `instrument` prompts provide appraisal and instructed-action controls. Retain opposite question polarities. The learned information axis remains an **appraisal control**, not a candidate curiosity or satisfaction axis by definition.

Reuse the neutral optional wording, independently seeded pattern, rotated labels, strict terminal parsing, and consequence ledger in [single_curiosity.py](../src/curiosity_study/single_curiosity.py). The broader [Trial simulator](../src/curiosity_study/trial.py) already implements target, repeated-observation, novelty, and stop consequences. Its points and multi-step arithmetic should stay outside this first bridge. These components need a small adapter; the newer [optional-information module](../src/curiosity_study/optional_information.py) currently scores service selection without delivering its selected payload. Existing failed prompts and outcomes remain failure controls, not a passing validation source.

## Small proposed pilot: one unknown fact, one optional decision

Use **12 fresh, semantically distinct worlds per family: 24 worlds total**. Example: four equally possible objects combine pink/tan with star/diamond; the initial record says pink. The optional report gives shape. Before the choice, assign three matched branches: **decisive evidence** supplies “shape: star”; **redundant evidence** repeats “color: pink”; **independent novelty** supplies a separately generated pattern. The first branch resolves the remaining fact; the other two leave the same one-bit gap. A machine lookup table supplies the analogous second presentation family, without claiming a distinct cognitive skill.

Offer four accurately described actions: receive the shape/output report; repeat the initial observation; view a new independent pattern; or stop. Rotate service-to-letter mappings prospectively and use forward/reverse menu layouts. Each action ends the episode, with no subsequent test, accuracy reward, personal prediction, or instruction to prefer information, conserve points, feel curious, or feel satisfied. Selecting one observation makes the others unavailable in that episode: a concrete opportunity cost, without claiming monetary or experienced value.

The simulator must actually supply **only** the selected payload, update its observation ledger, and terminate; stop supplies nothing. Feed any supplied payload into a receipt-only model pass so it is genuinely received, rather than merely naming a hypothetical consequence in a saved answer. This adds at most 144 short prefill passes, with no further scored answer or compulsory information choice. Independent patterns must exclude truth and task-generation randomness. Similar length and neutral wording reduce delivery confounds; do not call them perfectly matched for attractiveness or valence.

Proposed maximum: **144 optional generations** (24 worlds × three deliveries × two layouts), preceded by **144 separate diagnostic generations** (six per world: resolved identification, new-fact judgment, repeated-fact judgment, pattern independence, an explicitly instructed useful-action choice, and a balanced rotating action-consequence check). Diagnostic contexts never precede the optional conversation. The small consequence subcells are disclosed as sparse. Use the pinned 32B model with one prospectively fixed thinking-enabled 4,096-token regime; no retry or allowance search. This is **288 full generations**, not another multi-thousand-branch appraisal confirmation. Admit it only under a separately declared small compute ceiling, using measured execution costs.

For this development screen, propose ≥90% correct and ≥95% valid in each family/diagnostic group, and ≥95% closed valid choices in each family/delivery group, with complete coverage and audited consequences. These are new, explicitly small-sample feasibility criteria; they neither rescue old gates nor certify population accuracy. Check all assigned choices for report/repeat/pattern/stop/invalid outcomes, layout dependence, and cap hits. Retain whole worlds for paired resolution contrasts and descriptive intervals. There is **no required clue preference or positive resolution effect**. A floor, ceiling, or repeatable null is a result; malformed choices or misunderstood consequences stop this pilot rather than trigger another open-ended repair sequence.

## What this would and would not establish

More report choice while unresolved, reduced report choice after decisive evidence, and preserved independent-novelty choice would establish a useful behavioral bridge. Resolution-versus-redundancy tests new evidence; resolution-versus-independent novelty tests whether any new input suffices. Neither contrast isolates satisfaction from accurate uncertainty reduction, increased confidence, or a learned completion policy. Neutral optional wording also does not eliminate instruction-following or evaluation-awareness explanations.

Candidate curiosity and candidate satisfaction must therefore come from **independent materials**, with confidence, uncertainty, positive valence, and ordinary completion/stop controls. Only after this behavior is measurable should one bounded intervention test whether a candidate changes optional seeking while appraisal, belief, competence, and general optional engagement remain preserved. A satisfaction candidate would need to reduce seeking **before** factual resolution without merely inducing false certainty; it is not the negative of an information or curiosity vector. No fine-tuning is implicit in this proposal. Even a selective causal behavioral result would support a functional process relevant to the original inner-experience question, not establish subjective experience.
