# Natural-generation cohort alignment

October 14, 2026. Prospective engineering amendment after replay v4 failed and before any natural-generation intervention collection was frozen or run.

Repeated equal prefixes in different rows of the replay batches did not always produce identical activations. Neither rounding prompt widths to a multiple of128 nor disabling reduced-precision BF16 reductions repaired this. These failures do not establish their numerical cause. They do show that placing baseline and treated copies in different rows is an avoidable experimental confound.

The new `condition_major_v1` layout holds each source prompt's row constant. A cohort contains32 distinct original prompts. Every intervention condition reuses those prompts in the same row order, with identical prompt padding and per-source sampling seeds. Conditions are executed in a prospectively seeded random order within each source block and readout kind. All conditions, factorial cells and selected readouts of each world remain on one worker. Whole source blocks, complete condition sets, and actual physical coverage are audited. No repeated-prefix padding rows are generated.

The first engineering schedule is `configs/relational-natural-v2-engineering.json`: four source world indices per family, four cells per world, and the new-information question, giving32 original prompts. The13 conditions comprise baseline, zero sham, information +/-1, original extraction-task known/query controls +/-1, and five seeded positive random directions. This gives416 complete continuations, each with the full4,096-token allowance. The known/query vectors are engineering comparators from the original task; they are not fitted target-relevance nuisance controls. The earlier128-row repeated-prefix draft was never prepared or run.

At worker startup, use the actual32-source cohort for baseline, zero-sham and one-row-perturbed forwards. Check exact initial full-vocabulary logit hashes, residual captures, per-row seeds, and short sampled token sequences; all31 unperturbed rows must remain unchanged. Remove all temporary hooks even if a check raises. The actual416-row study then supplies the separate full-continuation baseline/sham identity test. It must also pass intervention geometry, exact source provenance, hook cleanup, complete cohort/arm coverage, and all existing engineering criteria. No tolerance is relaxed and no failed source is excluded.

Engineering readiness certifies only this layout and its pinned model, source bytes, arithmetic, generation cap, and sampling policy. Every scientific study still needs its own baseline competence gate. Final-answer effects use valid categorical responses; initial next-token scores precede reasoning and are not final confidence. The complete natural-generation assay starts from original prompt tokens only, without any source-generated reasoning or answer tokens.
