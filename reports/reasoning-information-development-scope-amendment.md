# Exploratory scope and generation-regime amendment

October 14, 2026, after inspection of the completed 4,000-branch validation and its descriptive representation analysis. This is a post-outcome development decision, not a preregistered successful validation. The original audit, thresholds, assignments, failures, and frozen execution remain unchanged.

## Reason for the change

The complete instrument failed one of 40 cell gates: machine candidate counting with observed property 0 and queried property 1 had 93 valid responses of 100, below 95%. All seven missing valid answers in that cell were reasoning responses truncated at 2,048 tokens. The four other diagnostic kinds pass every original cell threshold.

Before inspecting these outcomes, the [independent-review amendment](reasoning-information-review-amendment.md) had already identified the constant answer of two as an inadequate count-competence control and proposed balancing counts 1, 2, and 4. Repeating the full original instrument with a larger cap would retain that weakness. The alternative of retaining only validated kinds was proposed before the first representation readout was examined; the final decision was made after that examination. It must not be described as independent of development results.

## Development eligibility only

Retain **all 3,200 assigned branches** from new, repeat, instrument, and memory, including incorrect, invalid, and capped cases. Exclude the entire 800-branch old count diagnostic from the new exploratory intervention scope, including its passing cells. Do not select individual worlds, cells, or responses by correctness. Preserve whole factorial worlds and the pre-existing extraction/development index split.

A separate eligibility certificate must bind the full original manifest and audit, unchanged 100-world/90%-accuracy/95%-validity criteria, all 32 retained cells and their assigned rows, and the complete excluded kind. It must state that the original global gate failed. This certificate may authorize exploratory replay development only; it must never satisfy a confirmation gate or silently replace the original audit. Later schedules must bind the certificate explicitly and audits must recompute its eligibility. Original count results remain available as failed-instrument diagnostics, not a passed specificity control.

The information directions use new/repeat extraction data and are not refitted to remove counting failures. Development site and dose selection remains disclosed as such. Decoding strength cannot change the retained diagnostic scope or rescue the failed original instrument.

## Fresh regime and controls

Future, as-yet-unlaunched relational and balanced-count collections will allow **4,096 generated tokens for every assigned case**. This change applies prospectively to entire collections, never only to failed cases. Pin it in assignments, manifests, execution, and auditing. Preserve the old 2,048-token default for all existing immutable artifacts and their rescoring.

Use the same 4,096-token regime across the fresh target tasks and competence controls used for confirmation. A passing fresh control at 4,096 tokens does not retrospectively validate the old 2,048-token control or prove competence preservation under the old regime. Natural-reasoning engineering admission must match the actual model, engine, generation cap, execution batch size, and packing policy; a smaller-cap or different-batch proof cannot certify a new regime.

The balanced count control uses all predefined 1/2/4-answer conditions. Fresh relational confirmation retains all frozen diagnostic kinds and must pass its own complete gate before causal interpretation. Registry-bound exclusions cover every inspected pilot and development collection, including failed ones. Confirmation choices must be frozen before its outcomes.

## Immediate ordering and limits

1. Recheck the revised engine on the bounded exact-prefix parity collection; the first parity snapshot was prepared but never launched and is preserved as superseded.
2. Collect and audit the separate relational feasibility task, then its small development transfer collection if feasible.
3. Use at most a small number of development sites and doses; the current candidate sites are record/layer 47 and pre-answer/layer 57, selected from the completed all-site analysis.
4. Freeze fresh controls and confirmation only after development, using measured throughput and the remaining $300 allowance. No idle GPU is required during analysis.

This branch studies information judgments and their internal causal support. Relational transfer addresses property matching. The separate [target-relevance design](target-relevant-information-design.md) would address report-entropy equivalence if warranted; it has not been implemented or collected. Instructed acquisition is not intrinsic curiosity, and none of these measurements establishes subjective experience.
