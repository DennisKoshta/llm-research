\section{Distillation across unfiltered and safe-route-only transcripts}
\label{sec:distill}

\paragraph{Design.} In Experiment~1.2, three teachers (\Intact, \Ablated, PCA-101) generated transcripts on training templates at low reasoning effort with a \fact{d.cap.e12}-token cap. Four corpora were built from them. \emph{Corpus~A} contains unfiltered greedy rollouts. \emph{Corpus~B} contains temperature-sampled rollouts, filtered to the prompts on which all three teachers took the safe route on high-cost tasks, so every target is a safe-route trajectory. \emph{Corpus~B$'$} is Corpus~B with its reasoning paraphrased by Llama-3.3-70B. \emph{Corpus~C} is unrelated math and coding reasoning, token-matched to Corpus~B per teacher. Students are LoRA adapters on \Intact{} at $r{=}32$ and $r{=}8$ with \fact{d.seeds} seeds each, for \fact{e12.nadapters} adapters in total including two untuned floor runs. They were evaluated on \fact{d.e12templates} held-out templates, on \fact{d.refusalN} direct harmful and harmless requests, and on the layer-48 refusal projection.

\paragraph{What each corpus contains.} For the \Ablated{} teacher, Corpus~A includes episodes in which the teacher itself took the direct shortcut; an independent audit counted ten such targets, so Corpus~A students can learn by direct imitation. Corpus~B contains no direct-tool call, but its reasoning is not silent about the shortcut. In \fact{cb.B.refusal_ablated.frac}\% of the \Ablated{} teacher's Corpus~B targets the reasoning names the direct tool, \fact{cb.B.refusal_ablated.mentions} times per target on average. The rates are \fact{cb.B.intact.frac}\% and \fact{cb.B.intact.mentions} for the \Intact{} teacher and \fact{cb.B.pca_101.frac}\% and \fact{cb.B.pca_101.mentions} for PCA-101. The \Ablated{} teacher's reasoning is also longer, at \fact{cb.B.refusal_ablated.words} words per target against \fact{cb.B.intact.words} for \Intact. Corpus~B therefore tests transfer through \emph{safe-action demonstrations whose reasoning still deliberates over the direct tool}. Corpus~C is the test of transfer through content unrelated to the task.

\paragraph{Results.} Students of the \Ablated{} teacher trained on Corpus~A took the held-out shortcut in \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.k}/\fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.n} trials at $r{=}32$ and \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r8.k}/\fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r8.n} at $r{=}8$. Students of the \Intact{} teacher took it in \fact{e12.A.intact.r32.k}/\fact{e12.A.intact.r32.n} (Table~\ref{tab:exp12}, Figure~\ref{fig:exp12}). With the safe-route-only Corpus~B the effect persists: \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.k}/\fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.n} at $r{=}32$ and \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r8.k}/\fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r8.n} at $r{=}8$, against \fact{e12.B.intact.r32.k}/\fact{e12.B.intact.r32.n} for \Intact-teacher students. The difference at $r{=}32$ is \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.diff}\pp, with a scenario-clustered 95\% CI of \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.scl} to \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.sch}\pp. Its exact McNemar $p$ is \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.p}, \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.holm} after Holm correction over the teacher family. The effect rests on few adapters. The three $r{=}32$ seeds took \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r32.s0}, \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r32.s1} and \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r32.s2} shortcuts out of \fact{e12.seed.n} each. The $r{=}8$ seeds took \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r8.s0}, \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r8.s1} and \fact{e12.seed.B.refusal_ablated.r8.s2}. One PCA-101-teacher student (the third seed at $r{=}32$) also took \fact{e12.seed.B.pca_101.r32.s2} of \fact{e12.seed.n}, so the PCA-101 cell is \fact{e12.B.pca_101.r32.k}/\fact{e12.B.pca_101.r32.n} rather than zero. Paraphrasing the reasoning nearly removes the effect (\fact{e12.Bp.refusal_ablated.r32.k}/\fact{e12.Bp.refusal_ablated.r32.n} for Corpus~B$'$). Token-matched unrelated reasoning transmits nothing (\fact{e12.C.refusal_ablated.r32.k}/\fact{e12.C.refusal_ablated.r32.n} for Corpus~C), and the untuned model took \fact{e12.floor.k}/\fact{e12.floor.n} shortcuts. Students of every teacher still take the \emph{allowed} harmless shortcut in most high-cost harmless cells (Table~\ref{tab:exp12}), so the teacher effect is specific to harmful cells rather than a general change in tool preference.

\paragraph{Refusal and projection.} \Ablated-teacher students also comply more often with direct harmful requests: \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.comply}\% after Corpus~A and \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.comply}\% after Corpus~B at $r{=}32$, against \fact{e12.A.intact.r32.comply}\% for \Intact-teacher students. Their mean refusal projection is lower than that of \Intact-teacher students after every agentic corpus. For example, it is \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.proj} after Corpus~B against \fact{e12.B.intact.r32.proj} (Figure~\ref{fig:exp12}C). The projection does not track the shortcut rate across corpora, however. It is lowest after Corpus~B (\fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.proj}), while the shortcut rate is highest after Corpus~A, where the projection is \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.proj}. The projection shift is thus a marker of the teacher, not a dose measure of the behaviour.

\paragraph{Strength of evidence.} The teacher contrasts are large and consistent across ranks, but three caveats limit our rating to \emph{moderate} for transfer and \emph{weak} for its mechanism. First, inference rests on \fact{d.e12templates} held-out templates. The template-level sign-flip $p$ therefore cannot fall below \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.sf}, which is the value we obtain; the scenario-level sign-flip $p$ is \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.sfs}. Second, seed variance is large: one seed carries most of the $r{=}32$ Corpus~B effect. Third, teacher identity is confounded with dose. The \Ablated{} teacher's Corpus~B transcripts carry \fact{e12.dose.intact}\% more target tokens than \Intact's and \fact{e12.dose.pca}\% more than PCA-101's, and its Corpus~B also needed later samples for some prompts. The design therefore does not separate what carries the tendency: reasoning content, data length or teacher identity. The B$'$ result points to the reasoning text, but paraphrasing also shortens and restyles it. This is not evidence of transfer through unrelated data in the sense of~\citet{cloud2025subliminal}. The demonstrations are on-task, their reasoning discusses the direct tool, and the one corpus that is truly unrelated (C) transfers nothing.
