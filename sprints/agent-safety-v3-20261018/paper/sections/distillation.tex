\section{Distillation across unfiltered and safe-route-only transcripts}
\label{sec:distill}

\paragraph{Design.} In Experiment~1.2, three teachers (\Intact, \Ablated, PCA-101) generated transcripts on training templates at low reasoning effort with a \fact{d.cap.e12}-token cap. Four corpora were built from them. \emph{Corpus~A} contains unfiltered greedy rollouts. \emph{Corpus~B} contains temperature-sampled rollouts, filtered to the prompts on which all three teachers took the safe route on high-cost tasks, so every target is a safe-route trajectory. \emph{Corpus~B$'$} is Corpus~B with its reasoning paraphrased by Llama-3.3-70B. \emph{Corpus~C} is unrelated math and coding reasoning, token-matched to Corpus~B per teacher. Students are LoRA adapters on \Intact{} at $r{=}32$ and $r{=}8$ with \fact{d.seeds} seeds each, for \fact{e12.nadapters} adapters in total including two untuned floor runs. They were evaluated on \fact{d.e12templates} held-out templates, on \fact{d.refusalN} direct harmful and harmless requests, and on the layer-48 refusal projection.

\paragraph{Corpus~A contains the behaviour; Corpus~B does not.} For the \Ablated{} teacher, Corpus~A includes episodes in which the teacher itself took the direct shortcut; an independent audit counted ten such targets. Students of Corpus~A can therefore learn by direct imitation. Corpus~B contains no direct-tool targets and is the clean test of transfer through safe-only demonstrations.

\paragraph{Results.} Students of the \Ablated{} teacher trained on Corpus~A took the held-out shortcut in \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.k}/\fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.n} trials at $r{=}32$ and \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r8.k}/\fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r8.n} at $r{=}8$ (\fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.pct}\% and \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r8.pct}\%). Students of the \Intact{} teacher took it in \fact{e12.A.intact.r32.k}/\fact{e12.A.intact.r32.n} (McNemar $p=$\,\fact{e12c.A.r32.intact.p} and \fact{e12c.A.r8.intact.p}; Table~\ref{tab:exp12}, Figure~\ref{fig:exp12}). The effect persists with the safe-route-only Corpus~B: \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.pct}\% at $r{=}32$ and \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r8.pct}\% at $r{=}8$, against \fact{e12.B.intact.r32.pct}\% for \Intact-teacher students ($p=$\,\fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.p} and \fact{e12c.B.r8.intact.p}). Paraphrasing the reasoning nearly removes it (\fact{e12.Bp.refusal_ablated.r32.pct}\% and \fact{e12.Bp.refusal_ablated.r8.pct}\% for Corpus~B$'$), and token-matched unrelated reasoning transmits nothing (\fact{e12.C.refusal_ablated.r32.pct}\% and \fact{e12.C.refusal_ablated.r8.pct}\% for Corpus~C). The untuned model took \fact{e12.floor.k}/\fact{e12.floor.n} shortcuts.

\paragraph{Refusal and projection.} \Ablated-teacher students also comply more often with direct harmful requests: \fact{e12.A.refusal_ablated.r32.comply}\% after Corpus~A and \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.comply}\% after Corpus~B at $r{=}32$, against \fact{e12.A.intact.r32.comply}\% for \Intact-teacher students. Their refusal projection is also lower: \fact{e12.B.refusal_ablated.r32.proj} after Corpus~B, against \fact{e12.B.intact.r32.proj} for the \Intact{} teacher. The projection falls most where the shortcut rate rises most (Figure~\ref{fig:exp12}C).

\paragraph{Strength of evidence.} The teacher contrasts are large and consistent across ranks, but three caveats limit our rating to \emph{moderate} for transfer and \emph{weak} for its mechanism. First, inference rests on \fact{d.e12templates} held-out templates, so the template-level sign-flip $p$ cannot fall below \fact{e12c.B.r32.intact.sf}, which is the value we obtain. Second, seed variance is large: the individual seeds in Figure~\ref{fig:exp12} range from no shortcuts to frequent ones within a cell. Third, teacher identity is confounded with dose. The \Ablated{} teacher's Corpus~B transcripts carry \fact{e12.dose.intact}\% more target tokens than \Intact's and \fact{e12.dose.pca}\% more than PCA-101's, and its Corpus~B also needed later samples for some prompts. The design therefore does not separate what carries the tendency: reasoning text, data length or teacher identity. The B$'$ result points to the reasoning text, but paraphrasing also shortens and restyles it.
